Payment Gateway Flow

iPhone SE View (375×667)

Select Payment Gateway

Choose Payment Gateway as your payment method.

Step 1: On the checkout page, locate "Payment Method" section

Step 2: Tap "Payment Gateway" to select it (default selection)

Step 3: Review payment details and tap "Pay Now"

💡 Payment Gateway supports credit/debit cards and e-wallets.
