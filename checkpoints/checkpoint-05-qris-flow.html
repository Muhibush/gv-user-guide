QRIS Payment Flow

iPhone SE View (375×667)

1

Select QRIS Payment

Choose QRIS as your payment method.

Step 1: On the checkout page, locate "Payment Method" section

Step 2: Tap "QRIS" to select QR code payment

Step 3: Review payment details and tap "Pay Now"

💡 QRIS allows instant payment via QR code scanning from any Indonesian bank.

2 / 5
2

QR Code Display

View the QR code for payment.

Information Displayed:

  • Total Payment amount
  • Bank details (BCA)
  • Account number and name
  • QR code image

Actions:

  • "Download QR" - Save QR image
  • "I have paid" - Confirm payment

📝 Invoice status is PENDING until admin approval.

3 / 5
3

Download QR Code

Save the QR code image for sharing or later use.

Step 1: Tap "Download QR" button

Step 2: QR image opens in new tab

Step 3: Save the image to your device

💡 The downloaded QR includes branded design for sharing.

4 / 5
4

Pending Payment Status

After clicking "I have paid", the payment shows as pending.

Status: Payment is PENDING admin approval

Details:

  • Invoice number
  • Payment method
  • Expiration time
  • QR code for reference

Options:

  • "Download QR" - Save QR again
  • "Report via WhatsApp" - Contact support

📝 Admin typically confirms payment within 1-2 hours.

5 / 5
5

Purchase History

View all your purchases and their status.

Access: Tap "Purchase History" link or navigate to profile

Information Displayed:

  • Course name and type
  • Payment method
  • Invoice number
  • Status (Pending/Paid/Expired)
  • Amount paid

💡 Tap "Details" to expand and see full payment information.
