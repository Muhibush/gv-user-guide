Exercise Preference

Configure your exercise settings before starting.

Settings:

  • Soundscape (On/Off)
  • Vibration (On/Off)
  • Voice Guide (On/Off)
  • Buteyko Belt (On/Off)

Note: These preferences are asked once per exercise and can be changed anytime.

Action: Tap "Submit" to start the exercise

💡 Voice Guide is recommended for beginners to follow along.

Exercise Player

Start and follow your breathing exercise.

Interface Elements:

  • Exercise title (e.g., "Basic 1")
  • Progress indicators
  • BOLT score display
  • Control buttons (Play/Pause)

Actions:

  • Tap "Start" to begin exercise
  • Follow audio/visual cues
  • Complete the session

💡 BOLT (Body Oxygen Level Test) measures your breathing efficiency.
