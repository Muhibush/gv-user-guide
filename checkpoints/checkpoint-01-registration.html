Users select their wellness goals to personalize their experience.

Registration Form

Complete the registration form with personal details.

Required Fields:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Password
  • Date of Birth
  • City

Action: Fill in all fields and tap "Continue"

📝 All fields are required for account creation and verification.

8

Review & Confirm Details

Review all entered information before final submission.

Step 1: Review all personal details displayed

Step 2: Verify accuracy of name, email, phone, and other details

Step 3: Tap "This is correct" to proceed to verification

💡 Double-checking details prevents verification issues later.

9

Phone Verification (OTP)

Verify your phone number with a one-time password.

Step 1: Check your phone for the SMS verification code

Step 2: Enter the 6-digit code in the provided fields

Step 3: Tap "Verify" to complete phone verification

💡 The OTP code expires after a few minutes. Request a new code if needed.

10 / 11
10

Email Verification

Verify your email address with a one-time password.

Step 1: Check your email inbox for the verification code

Step 2: Enter the 6-digit code in the provided fields

Step 3: Tap "Verify" to complete email verification

💡 Check your spam folder if you don't see the verification email.
