Login Page

Access your existing BBB account with email and password.

Step 1: Enter your registered email address

Step 2: Enter your password

Step 3: Tap "Sign In" to access your account

💡 Use the "Forgot Password" link if you need to reset your password.

Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard provides access to all app features.

Key Sections:

  • Health Form Reminder
  • Consistency Tracking (Streaks)
  • The Practice (Course Types)
  • Recommended Events
  • Testimonials
  • Pro Trainers

Health Form

Complete your health questionnaire to personalize your experience.

Purpose: Helps tailor course recommendations based on your health needs

Step 1: Tap "Fill now" on the dashboard health form card

Step 2: Answer all health-related questions

Step 3: Submit the form to save your health profile

📝 Your health data is kept private and used only for personalization.
